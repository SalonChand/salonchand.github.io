About

I’m a computer engineering undergraduate at Himalayan Whitehouse International College in Kathmandu, graduating in 2027. I taught for three years before this — Computer Science and Mathematics, lesson materials and all — and I think it shows in what I build: nearly every project has an admin panel that someone non-technical has to survive on their own, so I spend real time on the boring screens.

I work on Windows, deploy to GitHub Pages, Vercel or the client's own machine, and I test on the mid-range Android phones my users actually own. I'd rather ship something plain that runs than something clever that needs babysitting — a fair amount of my work is going back and replacing the elegant version with the reliable one.

Open to internships and freelance work: front-end, full-stack, or the unglamorous integration work in between.
